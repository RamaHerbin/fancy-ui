Compare: the seam is now a blade of light. A white-hot core with a chromatic fringe and a glow that spills onto both pictures replaces the gradient line and star field, pulses of light run along it (faster while you interact), and moving the seam leaves a trail behind it that stretches with the speed of the gesture. The handle is a frosted glass bead with a ring of light. New `beamColors` (one or more colours) and `label` props. The slider now works from the keyboard (arrows, Shift+arrows, Page Up/Down, Home/End), autoplay eases at the ends, and in hover mode the seam glides back to its resting place instead of snapping. Reduced motion turns off the pulses, the trail and the glide. `StarField` is still exported but no longer used by Compare.
