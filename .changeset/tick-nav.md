Add TickNav: a navigation list on a ruler of tick marks. Bars near the pointer stretch and light up with a fading trail, the row under the pointer vibrates into a sine, labels ride their bar, and an optional preview card glides beside the active row. Keyboard focus drives the same effect; `sound` plays `tick` per row and `select` on activation.
