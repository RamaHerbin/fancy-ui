Add TickNav: a navigation list on a ruler of tick marks. Hovering a row plucks its line like a string, neighbouring lines and ticks stretch and light up on springs, and an optional preview card follows the pointer. Keyboard focus drives the same effect; `sound` plays `hover` per row. The motion lives in a framework-free core shared by the Svelte, React and Vue packages.
