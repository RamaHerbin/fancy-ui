# Inspiration media credits

Posters at the root of this folder (`<slug>.webp`) are captures of FancyUI's own components, taken by `scripts/build-inspiration-media.mjs`.

Files under `external/` show interactions from open-source projects. Every one of them is our own capture of the project's public demo, whose source lives in the project's repository under the licence listed below. We never copy images or videos from third-party sites, and a capture shows only the project's own interface (no third-party logos or photos). A reference whose licence or capture cannot be cleared this way stays a draft, with no media.
